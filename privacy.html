Privacy policy

With permission, it reads Philip's own Google Drive files, Google Calendar events and Google Contacts, and copies them to a private server he controls.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
